Page 5

Also, I want to share why I am so used to doing everything by myself. Growing up, I was put in an environment where no one really guided me or taught me how to navigate things properly. When I was a kid, my parents were rarely around, and I was often left alone inside the house with no one to talk to. We moved when I was very young, which separated me from my cousins, and I was not allowed to go outside because stepping out meant getting punished. Most of the time when my parents were around, the house was filled with hours of arguing and yelling. They still do that to this day, and it has become such a constant source of stress that I eventually had to pull away from that environment. That is also why I love staying at my grandparents house. Even when they have problems, they handle things peacefully and never project their frustrations onto anyone else. Whenever I tried to talk to my parents or ask for guidance, they would usually respond with anger, and sometimes they would even lock me outside for hours at night just because they were upset. Because of that, I learned to rely entirely on myself for everything. But because I was just trying to survive day to day, I missed out on learning the essential life skills I needed for my future. As I grew older, taking on my own responsibilities became overwhelming, and I often held myself back from trying new things simply because I did not know where to begin. Whenever I tried to share my ambitions, like my dream of pursuing esports, they would dismiss it as a waste of time. When I tried learning things on my own, they would often criticize or discourage me, making me doubt my own capabilities from an early age. That is also the biggest reason why I want to return to the Philippines so badly. I have a dream I am determined to build there, and leaving it behind remains my biggest regret. Before we moved here, I was just about to start taking steps toward my esports path, but I was told life would be better here. Instead of things getting better, they only got harder. And that is why I do not want to be with them anymore, because I am tired of seeing all these things for my entire life and nothing ever getting better. There is also something else I have been wanting to tell you, but I always struggle to find the right words or figure out how to explain it properly to you. I know this is not a very good explanation, but I hope you will understand. Happy 8th monthsary, and thank you for being my safe space through it all.
